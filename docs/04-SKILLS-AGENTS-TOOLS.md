# Skills, Agents, Tools and Sensors

**Skill:** versioned domain knowledge + procedure + validation.  
**Agent:** bounded reasoner/worker.  
**Tool:** external read/write capability.  
**Sensor:** continuous or scheduled observation source.

## Skill families
Search fundamentals; business discovery; intent/demand; crawling; indexation; rendering/JavaScript; technical SEO; architecture; on-page; content; internal linking; structured data; media; performance; local; ecommerce; international; authority; brand/entity; SERP; competitor; Search Console; analytics; Bing/IndexNow; AI Search/GEO/AEO; attribution; experimentation; policy; QA.

## Agents
Orchestrator, Discovery, Business Understanding, Technical, Rendering, Indexation, Demand, Content, On-page, Internal Linking, Schema, Performance, Local, Ecommerce, International, Authority, Competitor, SERP, AI Visibility, Analytics, Strategy, QA, Policy Guardian, Interpretation and Self-Audit.

Agents do not create competing independent strategies. Strategy Brain owns priority and objectives.

Every tool declares permissions, mutability, idempotency, rate limits, audit requirements, failure modes and rollback capability.
