# Client, Expert and Visual Brain Experience

## Client mobile
Primary client surface. Suggested navigation: **Home / Brain / Inbox / You**.

Home answers: Is my presence growing? What territory do I own? What is the Brain doing? Is anything at risk? Does it need my approval? What business impact is visible?

Brain Inbox surfaces only meaningful events: decision required, significant opportunity, significant risk, meaningful milestone and business result.

Technical jargon is translated into business meaning, with deeper evidence available on demand.

## Expert Console
Expose crawl/indexation, raw/rendered diffs, query-page maps, SERPs, competitors, authority, schema, performance, GSC/analytics, experiments, action queue, risk, approvals, deployment/rollback, agent traces, skill versions, evidence provenance, connector health and self-audit.

## Visual Parallel Brain
A living projection of the actual Search Knowledge Graph. Clusters may include Business, Customers, Market, Demand, Website, Google, Bing, AI Search, Content, Authority, Competitors, Locations, Conversions, Actions and Experiments. Motion must be subtle and driven by real events, not fake AI theatre.
