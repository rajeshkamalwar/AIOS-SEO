## 2. AIS-OS mechanisms and disposition

| Source / mechanism | Why it matters | Adopt / adapt / reject and AIOS application | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| README: Context → Connections → Capabilities → Cadence | Useful ordering of readiness dependencies | **Adapt:** Twin/evidence foundations → scoped sensors → tested SEO skills → durable scheduled cycles | URL-first provisional value must precede private connections; these are readiness concerns, not replacement domain layers |
| README: organized, discoverable skills and source routing | Persistent understanding must survive a fresh session | **Adapt:** released skill registry and evidence manifests; retrieval resolves exact claims rather than scanning arbitrary folders | A filesystem manual is not tenant authorization or temporal truth storage |
| Audit skill: verify routes, freshness and actual execution evidence | Configuration existence does not prove a healthy capability | **Adopt mechanism:** independent check receipts and explicit verification gaps in 21 | Public kit audit does not prove SaaS isolation, connector safety or SEO efficacy |
| Audit skill: retain dated findings and distinguish comparable baselines | Apparent improvement can come from changed scope or rubric | **Adapt:** versioned audit definitions, incident history and frozen evaluation cohorts | **Reject** one additive readiness score as an execution gate; safety failures cannot be averaged away |
| 3D Brain skill: explore real saved knowledge with categories and replay | Users need an understandable view of persistent context | **Adapt:** typed Search Knowledge Graph projection, evidence drill-down and real accepted-event history | **Reject** file adjacency as semantic truth and decorative activity; first validate accessible 2D/list interaction before committing to 3D |
| 3D Brain skill: prescribed globe/visual styling | Demonstrates one concrete visual treatment | **Reject as product constraint:** renderer and aesthetics remain replaceable | A globe can obscure scale, uncertainty or mobile usability; no copied assets/style dependency |

AIOS-specific proposal: use the four readiness concerns as questions at each rollout gate: Is business context supported? Are required observations actually available? Has this skill earned release? Is repeated execution bounded and monitored? A negative answer narrows the work. This is a readiness lens inside the existing Brain architecture, not a generic personal assistant model. Attribute Nate Herk's named framework when discussed; it is not AIOS branding.

## 3. AutoResearch mechanisms and disposition

| Source / mechanism | Why it matters | Adopt / adapt / reject and AIOS application | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| README: small editable experiment surface with fixed training budget | Bounded autonomy can be concrete and inspectable | **Adapt:** exact asset/treatment scope, cost reservation and stop conditions in each governed proposal | Search has delayed feedback and uncontrolled competitors; no fixed five-minute business outcome window |
| program.md: evaluator outside editable training file | The optimizer must not redefine success after results | **Adopt mechanism:** freeze evaluator/design before exposure, separate permissions and record versions | A prompt prohibition alone is insufficient; enforce through capabilities and independent evaluation ownership |
| program.md: baseline and logged keep/discard/crash iterations | Failed attempts inform future choices | **Adapt:** durable experiment/exposure/evaluation ledger including negative and inconclusive outcomes | Store typed failures, not zero-valued failed metrics; preserve history through compensation |
| program.md: keep an improved metric, otherwise revert | Clear bounded decision rule in its own environment | **Reject direct SEO transfer:** guardrails, uncertainty, delayed effects and confounders precede keep/iterate/revert recommendations | A ranking rise cannot establish causal benefit; reverting content does not reverse search exposure |
| program.md: indefinite repetition until interrupted | Demonstrates autonomous cadence | **Reject unbounded execution:** explicit budgets, authorization expiry, admission limits and safety stops | Repeated testing risks false discovery and site damage; no autonomous mutation in the first slice |

AIOS-specific proposal: in a future reviewed experiment, freeze eligible page clusters, treatment, baseline, metrics, interference assumptions and analysis. Record actual exposure and revisions; let an independent evaluator conclude beneficial, harmful, no detectable effect, inconclusive or invalid. A separate policy decision governs any resulting action. For low-data sites, a supported technical correction and honest observational follow-up may be more defensible than a statistically weak “experiment.”
