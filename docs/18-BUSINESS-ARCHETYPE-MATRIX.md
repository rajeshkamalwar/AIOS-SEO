# 18 — Business Archetype Matrix

Status: proposed activation contract. Capability identities and inherited contracts: [14](14-CAPABILITY-TAXONOMY.md), [15](15-CAPABILITY-CONTRACT.md). **Complexity belongs to the Brain.** Customers give a URL and correct material assumptions; they do not configure 195 toggles.

## 1. Activation is contextual, not a template strategy

An `ActivationAssessment` records tenant/business/site, Twin revision, capability contract version, archetype labels with evidence, page/locale scope, state, reason, unmet prerequisites and next review condition. Keep four dimensions separate: business relevance; evidence availability; released skill availability; execution authority. A disconnected source is relevant-but-unavailable, not irrelevant. An inferred local archetype can activate public investigation, not authorize a Business Profile edit.

All archetypes inherit the foundation: BUS/AUD discovery, bounded CRW/ROB/SMP/HTTP/URL/JS observation, MAP page classification, IA/LNK graph, applicable PAGE/CNT/SD/MED/PERF checks, POL/MEM/UX/AUDIT. Run only applicable page checks: no video does not mean a video defect. DEM/TER/SERP/COMP/BING/AI are activated when relevant search context can be observed; they do not generate fake demand when unavailable. ANA and GSC require actual connections. CHG/EXP become relevant with protected baselines or governed interventions; first-slice website actions remain disabled.

Core in the table means additional archetype-specific emphasis, not mandatory connected data at onboarding. Conditional means activate when its stated predicate is supported. Irrelevant means excluded under the stated business scope; a changed offering/Twin can reactivate it. Unlisted optional capabilities remain conditional on their applicability predicates, not silently disabled.

Table shorthand such as `LOC-06` resolves to `CAP-LOC-06`. Foundation capabilities still apply where meaningful. Business type does not exempt any tenant from policy, privacy or evidence requirements. Healthcare/education/financially consequential claims require qualified review; this is an AIOS governance constraint, not a claim about a universal platform ranking factor.

## 3. Resolution and priority procedure

1. Infer multiple archetype candidates from field-level evidence; retain disagreement. Start with safe foundation observations while uncertain.
2. Resolve offering/page/locale scope before activating specialist capability sets. A marketplace's company blog and seller inventory have different contracts.
3. Evaluate hard exclusions and policy first, then core/conditional relevance and evidence prerequisites. Missing permissions produce `unavailable:permission`, not `irrelevant`.
4. Select work by business relevance, evidence value, protection need and cost. An observation that reduces material uncertainty can outrank a speculative growth edit. No fixed archetype weight vector is claimed empirically optimal.
5. On confirmed business correction, new offering, closure or material strategy change, create a new activation revision and invalidate affected recommendations. Preserve old assessments and decisions.

Hybrid composition uses the union of relevant capabilities and intersection of constraints. Contradictory goals are not solved by running two independent strategy agents: the Strategy module records the conflict and chooses an explicit priority or asks one material question. A local retailer with online sales can have both visit and purchase goals, with distinct denominators and no double-counted transaction value.

## 4. Concrete acceptance examples

- A local plumber with a blog receives service-area and enquiry reasoning; generic national keyword volume cannot override unserved geography. Without Maps/GSC connections local visibility remains unknown.
- A Shopify-like catalog is not classified by its platform marker alone: observed product/variant pages activate commerce checks even when the platform cannot be identified. A marketing site built on the same platform need not activate stock analysis.
- A SaaS documentation page has successful support intent even if it does not convert directly. A conversion model cannot recommend pruning it solely for zero attributed sales.
- A publisher's paywalled article is not automatically thin because only a permitted excerpt was observed. Access limits propagate to usefulness/duplication conclusions.
- A clinic page with a city name cannot activate new-city publishing or infer licensure. Public business inference and qualified clinical validation remain separate.

Acceptance requires these cases, a multi-location closure, a marketplace duplicate listing and a hybrid retailer to produce different activation/priority records using the same domain contract. Labels, expected outputs and test corpus are Phase 3 artifacts; no models have been evaluated here.
