# Canonical Vision

The customer expectation is simple: SEO should help the right people discover the business, increase relevant visibility and contribute to enquiries or sales. The customer should not need to understand canonicals, schema, crawl budgets, transactional keywords or authority jargon.

Onboarding begins with **one URL**. The system infers business type, offerings, likely customers, locations, site technology, page inventory, content, entities, competitors and demand. Search Console, analytics and execution connections deepen the Brain progressively but should not block first value.

The Brain is not a collection of automations. Automations are its reflexes and tools. The Brain has continuity: it observes, remembers, reasons, prioritizes, acts, evaluates and changes future decisions.

Strategy is business-aware. Local, ecommerce, SaaS, B2B, multi-location and content businesses require different territory and opportunity models.

A beautiful site may still be a weak search asset. The Brain sees raw HTML, rendered DOM, crawler accessibility, structured data, performance, internal architecture, content usefulness and conversion paths.

SEO, local, AEO/GEO, AI search, content, authority and technical work are capabilities under **Search Growth**, not disconnected products.

The client experience is primarily a polished mobile application. The cloud Brain remains active while the app is closed. Browser and Expert Console surfaces expose deeper exploration and operations.
